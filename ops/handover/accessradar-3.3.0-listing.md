No new scopes. Installs automatically. Runs on Atlassian, zero egress. Signed evidence format unchanged. No UI change.

- Adds NIS2 Art. 21(2)(i) (human resources security, access control policies and asset management) and Art. 21(2)(f) (policies and procedures to assess the effectiveness of cybersecurity risk-management measures). Other Art. 21(2) points are not about Jira access and are not mapped.

- The mapping is a supporting interpretation, not certification or an auditor's opinion. It supports evidence collection. It is not a statement of compliance and is not legal advice.
